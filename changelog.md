## 1.0

- First release.
- Streams an MP3 from the SD card to an ESP32 WiFi devboard, which re-broadcasts
  it as a live audio stream over its own access point.
- Listeners join the network and press play in a browser. Nothing to install.
- Captive portal hands phones the address by itself when they join.
- Pause and resume without losing your place in the track.
- Live listener count, link health and buffer level on the Flipper screen.
- Rename your station from Settings so people see your name in the WiFi list.
- Up to eight listeners at once.
