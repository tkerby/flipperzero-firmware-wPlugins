Turns the Flipper into a remote that a Kindle accepts as a Kindle Click: page forward and back, light up and down, and sleep. The Kindle also shows the Flipper's battery level.

## Keys

- Right / Left: page forward / back
- Up / Down: light up / down (repeats while held)
- OK, held 1 s: the Kindle sleeps
- Back: menu (Remote, Buttons, Forget Kindle); Back again exits

Buttons in the menu lets you assign any key (including a short press of OK) to page forward, page back, light up, light down, sleep, or off.

Keep the app open while you read. While it runs, the Flipper's own Bluetooth (for the phone app) is off.

## Pairing

Open the app, then on the Kindle open Settings › Wi-Fi & Bluetooth › Bluetooth devices and choose the Kindle Click. The Kindle and the Flipper show the same code: confirm it on both.

To pair again, forget it on the Kindle (Bluetooth devices › the remote › Forget), then choose Forget Kindle in the app's menu.

## Limits

- No remote can wake the Kindle. After you wake it, the app reconnects within a few seconds, and a page turn pressed meanwhile is delivered once the Kindle is ready.
- The Kindle's sleep timer still runs from the last screen touch: it doesn't count remote page turns as activity (Kindle firmware 5.20.1).
- Only Kindle models with Kindle Click support accept it. Tested on a Kindle Paperwhite 12th gen.
